Learn a variety of skills

The skills you learn Digital Technology are transferrable to any and every profession industry.

Design and Develop Digital Outcomes

By allowing you to focus on contexts and areas that you are passionate about, learning to design, create, refine and develop digital outcomes that are epxressions of who you are make learning much more fun. Understanding how our creations affect the people and world around is a major part of developing outcomes that have real impact.

Computational Thinking

Breaking down and understanding each individual working part is key to working efficiently and learning how to manipulate a system to best suit your needs. Problem-olving is one of the most important skills for any job, and learning how to best use the tools that you have at your disposal is essential for success in every career.

Project Management

Planning may not seem like to most interesting skill, but being an efficient planner, adjusting a project as needed and decomposing a big project into smaller parts is one of the most useful skills employers are looking for. Setting and meeting deadlines is just as important as actually doing something, and the proceses we have in Digital Technology help you build your management capabilites.
